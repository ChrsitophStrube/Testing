Replace the forming set

You may need to replace the forming set when you want to produce bags with a different style or size.

WARNING

Heavy object

A forming set can weigh up to 30 kg.

Before you begin

Follow the instructions below.

  1. Push the stop button to stop the packaging machine.
  2. Open the doors of the form, fill and seal section.
  3. Get access to film transport module, longitudinal-seam sealing module and forming set.

Procedure

  2. Pull the indicated handle towards you and slide the right part of the film transport module to the right.
  4. Pull the indicated handle towards you and slide the left part of the film transport module to the left.
  6. Loosen the indicated clamps and remove the funnel.
  8. Loosen the indicated turning knobs.
  10. Replace the forming set.
  12. Place the funnel back and fasten the indicated clamps to lock the funnel.
  14. Fasten the indicated turning knobs.
  16. Slide the right part of the film transport module against the forming tube and push the indicated handle away from you.
    Note that the right part moves slightly away from the forming tube to automatically set the correct distance between film transport module and the forming tube.
  18. Slide the left part of the film transport module against the forming tube and push the indicated handle away from you.
    Note that the left part moves slightly away from the forming tube to automatically set the correct distance between film transport module and the forming tube.
  19. Close the doors of the form, fill and seal section.

Result

You can now produce bags with a different style or size.
