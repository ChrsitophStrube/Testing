Replace the longitudinal-seam sealing jaw

You must replace the longitudinal-seam sealing jaw when the recipe specifies a different longitudinal-seam sealing jaw.

WARNING

Hot surface

The surface can reach a temperature of 240 °C (464 °F).

  1. Wear gloves that are resistant to the high temperature.
  2. Wait for the longitudinal-seam sealing jaw to cool down after you removed the electrical and pneumatic connectors.

WARNING

Heavy load

The longitudinal-seam sealing jaw can weigh up to 5 kg.

Before you begin

  1. Push the stop button to stop the packaging machine.
  2. Open the doors of the form, fill and seal section.

Procedure

  2. Turn the indicated handle that holds the longitudinal-seam sealing bar.
  4. Move the indicated bracket slightly backwards and hold it.
  6. Open the suspension bracket sidewards and turn the suspension bracket as far as possible until it locks automatically.
    Make sure the locking pin locks the suspension bracket that holds the longitudinal-seam sealing module.
  8. Guide the indicated bracket back to its original position to lock the door and prevent accidentally closing.
  10. Remove the electrical and pneumatic connectors.
    Wait for the longitudinal-seam sealing jaw to cool down after you removed the electrical and pneumatic connectors.
  12. Use the indicated handle to lift and remove the longitudinal-seam sealing bar.
  14. Use the indicated handle to place and lower the longitudinal-seam sealing bar.
  16. Connect the electrical and pneumatic connectors again.
  18. Lift the locking pin and close the suspension bracket again.
  20. Turn the indicated handle to lock the longitudinal-seam sealing bar.
  21. Close the doors of the form, fill and seal section.

Result

You can now use the newly installed longitudinal-seam sealing jaw.
